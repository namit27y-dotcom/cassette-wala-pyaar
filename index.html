YAADEIN — 90s NOSTALGIA VAULT ↴
1989 — 1994 · RAJSHRI ERA

The Voice of Prem: SPB & Salman Khan

Before six-pack abs and action thrillers, Salman Khan was the floppy-haired, soft-spoken romantic hero in black leather jackets. S.P. Balasubrahmanyam gave Prem his soulful, effortless voice in Maine Pyar Kiya and Hum Aapke Hain Koun — making entire generations fall in love with "Mere Rang Mein Rangne Wali".

1990 — 1998 · GOLDEN CASSETTES

The Kumar Sanu Gold Rush

There was a time when every paan shop, bus stand, and barber shop in India had Kumar Sanu playing on a T-Series tape. Recording a world-record 28 songs in a single day, his voice turned Nadeem-Shravan and Jatin-Lalit melodies into eternal emotional anthems.

VIVIDH BHARATI · MIDNIGHT

Transistor Radios & Kishore Da

Listening to Kishore Kumar on a red Murphy radio with low battery cells, waiting through static for "Dekha Ek Khwab" or "Mere Samnewali Khidki Mein". Kishore Da’s magic didn’t need auto-tune; it needed a room with the lights off and someone you missed.

ANALOG MEMORIES · 90s DIARY

The Natraj Pencil & Rewound Tapes

Saving Walkman batteries by spinning the cassette spools with a Natraj hexagonal pencil. Sticking transparent scotch tape over the write-protect notch to record over an old lecture tape. Writing tracklists with Reynolds pens on folded paper J-cards.
